I am a student at **North China Electric Power University (Beijing)** focused on **AI Agent Workflow Engineering**: turning LLMs, tool calling, multi-step task orchestration, and automation into pipelines that truly run. Rather than single-model benchmarks, I care about whether the whole workflow runs reliably, is observable, and keeps improving.

I maintain projects around agents and automation on GitHub, with experience in workflow orchestration, toolchain integration, automation scripting, and engineering delivery. Featured projects are under active construction — collaboration and discussion are welcome.

## Interests

- **Agent Workflow Engineering**: multi-agent orchestration, task decomposition, tool calling, state machines
- **LLM Application Engineering**: prompt engineering, RAG pipelines, observability, failure recovery
- **Automation & Integration**: CI/CD, scripting, third-party service orchestration
- **AI Infrastructure**: model serving, vector stores, caching, high-availability architectures
- **Human-AI Interfaces**: packaging agent capabilities into usable product surfaces
